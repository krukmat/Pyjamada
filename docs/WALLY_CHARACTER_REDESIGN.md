# Wally Character Redesign — W1

Status: **character study / no animation production yet**

## Why this exists

The first generated arcade Wally proved the runtime path but failed the visual review. It read as a generic, rigid retro avatar rather than the protagonist Pyjamada needs. The rejected bedroom A2 also confirmed that technique must not outrank character identity.

## Character target

Wally should read, at gameplay scale, as:

- sleepy before heroic;
- sympathetic and slightly awkward;
- domestic rather than action-heroic;
- immediately recognizable from hair, face and pajamas;
- expressive with very few pixels;
- compatible with a warm 16-bit game without looking like a generic 8-bit mascot.

## What must survive simplification

1. Brown hair with an identifiable silhouette, not a square cap.
2. A readable face with sleepy / worried / startled potential.
3. Blue pajamas with a warm yellow accent.
4. Small slippers and slightly awkward stance.
5. Enough head and hand pixels to support expressive animation later.

## What to avoid

- oversized square head with a blank face;
- perfectly symmetric hair and body;
- thick outlines everywhere at the expense of facial expression;
- producing a full atlas before the base idle design is approved;
- copying any existing commercial character.

## W1 static candidates

### A — Retroized original
Closest to the current character identity. Softer head silhouette, drooped sleepy eyes, visible nose, asymmetric hair, relaxed shoulders.

### B — Expressive 16-bit
Slightly smaller head and longer body. More facial pixels, clearer fringe and hands. Intended to support richer startled / attack / interaction poses.

### C — Compact charm
Shorter and friendlier silhouette, larger sleepy face, short legs and stronger pajama read. Most stylized of the three.

## Approval rule

Do not animate any candidate until one static design is selected in the same A1 bedroom context and at gameplay scale.
